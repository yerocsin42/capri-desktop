## What she does

- **Talks back.** Type to her and she answers in her own words, with a free AI brain that runs on your PC.
- **Talks out loud.** Add your own ElevenLabs voice and she speaks. Tap the wave for a full voice chat.
- **Sits on your stuff.** Drop her on a window, a desktop icon or the bottom of your screen.
- **Has moods.** She flails when you toss her, glitches when you poke her and naps when you leave her alone.

## Getting started

1. Download **Capri-Setup.exe** and run it
2. In the Capri Mainframe, press **Set up her brain**
3. Optional: paste an ElevenLabs key for her voice

Windows or your antivirus may warn about it because it's a new, unsigned app. All the code is here.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Pencil | type to her |
| Wave | voice chat |
| Mic button | mute your mic while chatting |
| "Open Spotify" | she opens apps, games and sites, and skips songs |
| "Play Blinding Lights" | she plays it on Spotify, or YouTube without it |
| "Close Discord" | she switches to, minimizes or closes open apps and tabs |
| Watch my screen | in the Mainframe: she comments as you play |
| Speak replies | in the Mainframe: off saves ElevenLabs credits |
| Free voice chat | in the Mainframe: talk without ElevenLabs, she types back (69 MB download on first use) |
| Drag | sit her on a window |
| Shift + drop | sit her right there |
| Ctrl + drop | she hangs on right there; click her to let go |
| Wander around | right-click her, Move: she hops between your open windows |
| Ctrl + Alt + V | start or stop voice chat |
| Ctrl + Alt + N | mute your mic |
| Ctrl + Alt + C | get her off your cursor |
| Tray icon | menu, Mainframe, quit |
