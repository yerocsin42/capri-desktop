2Her voice ElevenLabs

She talks and listens through ElevenLabs.

Account

  1. Sign up at elevenlabs.io

Design her voice optional

  1. ElevenCreative → Voice Creation → Voice Design
  2. Paste this prompt, then create the voice

    cybernetic hologramic sounding woman playful voice is light, bright, and buoyant, with a lilt that lifts at the ends of her phrases as though she’s teasing or in on a joke only she understands. Her pacing is quick and bouncy, slipping between mock-innocent sweetness and a sly, knowing edge that keeps listeners slightly off balance. She sounds close and airy, almost whispered into the ear, which suits an AI construct who exists only inside the heads of those who plug in her chip, and a faint digital sheen, a flicker of glitch or shimmer, tends to creep in when her mischief sharpens. Beneath the performance, the act can crack, letting a quieter, more vulnerable tone slip through that hints at the Project Capricorn past she can’t quite escape.

  3. Voices → My Voices → ⋯ → Copy Voice ID

API key

  1. Bottom left: switch ElevenCreative to ElevenAPI
  2. API Keys → Create Key, name it Capri
  3. Permissions:
    Text to Speech
    Access
    Voices and Models
    Read
    Agents
    Write
  4. Create Key, then copy it before you close the popup

No Agents option, or the key fails? Turn Restrict key off.

In the Mainframe

  1. Paste the key → Connect
  2. Paste your Voice ID over the one in the box below it, or keep it
  3. Test

Stuck?

“ElevenLabs rejected the key”
That isn't the key. Keys start with sk_. Make a new one and paste it.
“Key needs … access”
Fix the key's permissions, or turn Restrict key off.
“Voice not found”
Paste the Voice ID from My Voices, not the voice's name.
“Out of ElevenLabs credits”
Credits refill each month. She uses about one per letter she says.
“Free tier usage disabled”
Turn off your VPN and try again.
She can't hear you
Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → let desktop apps use it.
Antivirus flags it
A false alarm on a new, unsigned app. Check the code on GitHub.
